# Hearthfield Idle: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers the game on the web, on Android and iOS, and on Steam._

**This game collects no data.**

## What the game stores

- Your farm (the save file) and your settings (volume, interface size, camera) are stored **only on your device**: in your browser's storage on the web, and in the app's own storage on phones, tablets and computers.
- When you choose **Export save** or **Save to a file** in Settings, the game hands the save to you as text or a file. Where it goes from there is up to you.
- On Steam, Steam Cloud may copy the save file between your own computers. That is a Steam feature, covered by Valve's privacy policy; the game itself sends nothing.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no email address.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no in-app purchases.
- No network access: the game never contacts a server while you play. The web version is downloaded from GitHub Pages like any web page, and may keep a copy so it also starts offline.
- No personal information is collected, shared or sold, from adults or children.

## Deleting your data

Use **Hard reset** in Settings, uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
